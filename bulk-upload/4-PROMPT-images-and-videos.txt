IMAGE POSTS AND VIDEOS - UPLOAD MANY AT ONCE
============================================
IMAGE POST  = one or more pictures (up to 10) that learners swipe through, with an optional caption. Good for notes, formula
              sheets, diagrams, chapter summaries.
VIDEO       = one short MP4 file.

SIMPLE STEPS
1. Put all your pictures / videos in ONE folder on your phone (for example Downloads/batch-1). Do NOT rename them after step 3.
2. Open an AI chat. Copy everything between "COPY FROM HERE" and "COPY UNTIL HERE" below, paste it, and then write the list of
   your files: one line per post, with the exact file names, the title and the topic (see the example at the bottom).
   Tip: you can also type the file names as a list by looking at your folder.
3. The chat returns a manifest (.json). Save it into the same folder. The file must end with .json.
4. In the app: Create -> "Import many (file)" -> choose the manifest.
5. Tap "Select media files" and select ALL the pictures and videos of that manifest at once (long-press one, then tap the others).
6. Check the summary: items ready, MISSING files, unused files, wrong type or too big. Fix or ignore them (affected items are skipped).
7. Tick the box (the content is yours or you may share it) and tap "Start import". Keep the app open. Videos take longer.
   If it stops, it shows the item number to continue from. Type it in "Start from item number" and start again.

LIMITS
- Pictures: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. Up to 10 pictures per image post.
- Videos: MP4 only, up to 60 MB each. Other formats (MOV, MKV, AVI) are not accepted - convert them to MP4 first.
- One manifest: up to 2000 items and 600 files. For videos, 20 to 50 per manifest is comfortable.
- Normal accounts can post about 20 items per hour. Trusted creators about 1000 per hour.
- If the same file is used in two posts, it is uploaded twice (each post needs its own copy).

FILE NAMING ADVICE
- Use simple names: lowercase letters, numbers and dashes. Good: newton-page1.jpg, reel-01.mp4. Hindi names also work.
- Keep names unique: never two different files with the same name. The app matches names exactly (capital or small letters
  do not matter, the folder does not matter).
- Number them (reel-01, reel-02 ...) so the order is easy to follow.

HOW TO MAKE FILES SMALLER
- Pictures: export as JPG with quality 80 percent, or as WebP; a width of 1080 px is enough. Phone photos (4000 px) are often
  4 to 8 MB - resize them.
- Videos: record in 720p or 1080p at 30 fps, not 4K. Use your phone's "HD, 30 fps" setting. A 60-second 1080p video is about 20 to
  40 MB. If a video is above 60 MB, compress it with a free app (for example "Video Compress" or CapCut with 720p export) or
  cut it into two parts. Short videos (15 to 60 seconds) work best.
